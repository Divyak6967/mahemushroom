Our Story

Grown Close to Home, Shared with Care

MaheMushroom is a local farm with a simple purpose: wholesome food that brings natural goodness to your everyday meals.

01The Name

A Name with a
Person Behind It

MaheMushroom is named after our founder, Mahe. Putting a name on the farm is a promise: every pack that leaves us carries that name — and the care that comes with it.

04Nothing Goes to Waste

After the Harvest,
a Second Life

Once a batch of mushrooms is harvested, the material it grew on still has value. We turn it into compost — so it goes back to the land instead of being thrown away.

  1. The harvest ends

    When a batch has finished growing, the used growing material is cleared from the grow room.

  2. It becomes compost

    Rich in organic matter, it is turned into compost rather than going to waste.

  3. Back to the land

    The compost goes to farmers nearby, and is also sold as compost.
