Our Process

From Farm to Fresh

  1. Grow
    Naturally

    Our mushrooms grow in a clean grow room, looked after with care every single day.

  2. Harvest at
    the Right Time

    Each batch is picked when it's at its best — not too early, not too late.

  3. Clean & Pack
    Hygienically

    Produce is cleaned, checked and packed hygienically, ready for your kitchen.

  4. Delivered
    Fresh to You

    We deliver locally, so the journey from our farm to your door stays short.
